# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 1, 2026

ZagJobSearch ("we," "us," or "our") operates zagjobsearch.vercel.app (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## 1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect:
- **Full Name** — to personalize your experience
- **Email Address** — for login and essential notifications
- **Password** — stored securely as a hash (we never see it)
- **Date of Birth** — to verify you are old enough to use the Service
- **Country** — to filter job results to your location

When you use the Service, we collect:
- **Search queries** — to show you relevant jobs and improve results
- **Bookmarked jobs** — so you can save jobs for later
- **Search history** — so you can revisit past searches

We do **not** collect:
- Phone numbers
- Physical addresses
- Payment information
- Government IDs

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Provide job search results based on your country and query
- Save your search history and bookmarks
- Send essential account notifications
- Improve the Service over time

We do **not** sell your data. We do **not** share it with advertisers.

## 3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third parties:
- **Supabase** — for secure authentication and database storage
- **Adzuna** — for job listings (when you search)
- **Himalayas** — for remote job listings (when you search)
- **Vercel** — for hosting

These providers have their own privacy policies.

## 4. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access** your data
- **Correct** inaccurate data
- **Delete** your account and all associated data
- **Export** your data in a portable format
- **Withdraw consent** at any time

To exercise these rights, email us at: **blesszaglago@gmail.com**

## 5. Data Security

We use industry-standard security:
- Passwords are hashed (never stored in plain text)
- All connections use HTTPS
- Database access is restricted with row-level security
- No session replay or invasive tracking

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your data within 30 days.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for anyone under 13. We block registration for users under 13. If we learn a user under 13 has registered, we delete their data immediately.

We may update this policy. Material changes will be notified by email.
